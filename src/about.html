About Us

Welcome to our website! On-Demand Interactive Course Assistant, or "OCA", is an OpenAI based chat system designed to help you understand software requirements, design, and development processes.

On our Home page, you can speak directly to OCA in either Tutoring or Conceptual mode. The Lectures page has the notes OCA refers to and is available for you to access.

We hope our content helps you grasp key ideas quickly and apply them to your projects!
